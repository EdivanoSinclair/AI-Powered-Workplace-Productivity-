Workplace Productivity Assistant

A practical collection of small tools to help with everyday workplace writing, meeting follow-up, task planning and research preparation.

Prototype notice

This version runs in your browser using JavaScript rules and templates. It does not connect to a live AI model, send your text to a server, or perform live web research. Treat outputs as drafts and verify them before using them at work.

Responsible AI checklist

  • Remove personal, confidential and company-sensitive information before entering notes.
  • Review every generated draft for accuracy, tone and missing context.
  • Confirm decisions, deadlines, names and action owners with the original source.
  • For research, use reliable sources and independently check citations and claims.
  • Use human judgement for important workplace, employment, security or customer decisions.
